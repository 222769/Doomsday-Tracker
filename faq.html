Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?

In your browser's local storage, on this device only. Clearing your browser data, using a different browser, or switching devices will reset the checklist.

What's the difference between release order and timeline order?

Release order is the order titles actually came out. Timeline order is the in-universe chronological order. Use the toggle above the checklist to switch between them at any time.

What does a "stop point" mean?

A few shows only have a portion of their run listed as required viewing. When an item has a stop point, it's shown right under the title — for example, "watch through S1E4 only."

Can I use this offline?

Yes. Watch Tracker is an installable app (PWA). After your first visit, the app shell is cached so it keeps working without a connection, and your checked-off progress is read from local storage either way.

How do I add my own list?

The whole checklist comes from a single data.js file. If you're comfortable editing a bit of JavaScript, you can swap in your own titles, order numbers, and runtimes.

Is this affiliated with any studio?

No. Watch Tracker is an independent, fan-made project with original sample content, built purely as a personal watch-order tool.
