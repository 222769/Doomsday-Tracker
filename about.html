About Watch Tracker

Watch Tracker is a small, independent checklist for working through every movie and show on the road to Doomsday — in the right order, with stop points marked — before the big day arrives. It started as a personal spreadsheet and turned into this single-page app.

Everything runs entirely in your browser. There's no account, no server, and no analytics — your progress is saved to your device's local storage and never leaves it.

Why build this?

Long-running shared universes are fun to marathon, but keeping track of release order versus story order (and which side-show episodes actually matter) gets tedious fast. This app just keeps that list in one place.
